## Methods

- [Morning Pages](https://juliacameronlive.com/) - Three pages of longhand, first thing in the morning, about anything at all. From Julia Cameron's *The Artist's Way*.
- [Bullet Journal](https://bulletjournal.com/) - Ryder Carroll's system of short, bulleted logging in a paper notebook, with an index and a monthly review.
- [Interstitial journaling](https://nesslabs.com/interstitial-journaling) - A line or two, with the time, between one task and the next. From Ness Labs.
- [One Line a Day](https://www.chroniclebooks.com/collections/one-line-a-day) - Five-year journals with room for one short entry a day, so each page shows the same date across five years.
- [Commonplace book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonplace_book) - A notebook of quotes, ideas and things worth keeping, a practice going back centuries.
- [Dream diary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_diary) - Writing your dreams down as soon as you wake up.
- [Expressive writing](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1997.tb00403.x) - Writing about something that affected you, for 15 to 20 minutes on a few days in a row. The method behind the studies in [Research](#research).

## Apps

- [Daymint](https://daymint.app) - iPhone and iPad. One page a day: open it and it's today's page. Old pages come back on their own as finds. Writing is free.
- [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com) - A full-featured journal with photos, places and templates, on Apple devices and Android.
- [Apple Journal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/journal/id6447391597) - Apple's free journaling app for iPhone, with suggestions drawn from your photos, places and workouts.
- [Diarium](https://diariumapp.com) - A diary for Windows, Android and Apple devices that can pull in your calendar, photos and other activity.
- [Penzu](https://penzu.com) - An online journal you write in the browser.
- [Obsidian daily notes](https://obsidian.md/help/plugins/daily-notes) - A note for each day in Obsidian, kept as plain Markdown files.

## Open source

- [jrnl](https://jrnl.sh) - A journal for the command line. Entries are plain text, with optional encryption. ([source](https://github.com/jrnl-org/jrnl))
- [Logseq](https://logseq.com) - An outliner that opens on today's journal page. ([source](https://github.com/logseq/logseq))

## Paper

- [Hobonichi Techo](https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/) - A page-a-day planner and diary from Japan, with a short quote at the foot of every page.
- [Leuchtturm1917](https://www.leuchtturm1917.com) - Notebooks with numbered pages and a blank index, a common choice for a Bullet Journal.
- [Moleskine](https://www.moleskine.com) - Hardcover notebooks and dated diaries.
- [Field Notes](https://fieldnotesbrand.com) - Pocket notebooks for a few lines on the go.

## Books

- [The Artist's Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Artist%27s_Way) - Julia Cameron's twelve-week course for writers and artists, built on Morning Pages.
- [The Bullet Journal Method](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/book) - Ryder Carroll on the system and the thinking behind it.
- [Writing Down the Bones](https://nataliegoldberg.com/books/) - Natalie Goldberg on timed writing practice: keep your hand moving and don't cross out.
- [Journal of a Solitude](https://wwnorton.com/books/9780393309287) - May Sarton's journal of a year living and writing alone in New Hampshire.

## Diaries worth reading

- [The Diary of Samuel Pepys](https://www.pepysdiary.com) - Pepys's diary of 1660s London, published online one day at a time.
- [Meditations](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2680) - Marcus Aurelius's notes to himself, free on Project Gutenberg.
- [Anne Frank's diary](https://www.annefrank.org/en/anne-frank/diary/) - The Anne Frank House on the diary and its history.
- [Thoreau's Journal](https://thoreau.library.ucsb.edu/writings_journals.html) - Henry David Thoreau kept a journal for 24 years. The Thoreau Edition is publishing all of it.

## Research

- [Pennebaker (1997), Writing About Emotional Experiences as a Therapeutic Process](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1997.tb00403.x) - *Psychological Science*. A summary of the first decade of expressive-writing studies.
- [Smyth (1998), Written emotional expression: Effect sizes, outcome types, and moderating variables](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-006X.66.1.174) - *Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology*. An early meta-analysis.
- [Frattaroli (2006), Experimental disclosure and its moderators: A meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.6.823) - *Psychological Bulletin*. A larger meta-analysis, which found real but small effects on average.
- [James W. Pennebaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_W._Pennebaker) - The psychologist behind most of this work.
